The estuary is listening

Focus the world view, then use WASD or arrow keys to travel. Press and hold Shift while moving to brace the load. Press Space to scan, E to interact, V to switch Chart 2D and Relief 3D, and Escape to cancel a destination. In Relief 3D, hold J or L, right-drag, or Alt-drag to orbit; on touch, twist with two fingers. The compass arrow always points toward world north.
